Monthly and per-paycheck budgets, 50/30/20, variable-income mode, sinking funds, debt payoff (snowball or avalanche) and savings goals for 2027.

What's inside

  • Monthly view: pick a month to see budget vs actual for every category and your 50/30/20 split against target (percentages adjustable).
  • Paycheck view: weekly, biweekly, twice-monthly or monthly pay. It lists the bills due before each next payday, shows what's left, and highlights months with an extra paycheck.
  • Variable-income mode: for freelancers and commission earners. Tax is set aside first, you pay yourself a steady salary, and the rest goes to a buffer for slow months, with a months-of-buffer status.
  • Sinking funds: the monthly amount needed to hit each irregular cost (insurance, holidays, car repairs) by its due date.
  • Debt payoff: up to 8 debts, Snowball or Avalanche, extra payments that roll over as debts are paid off, a month-by-month schedule (up to 10 years), payoff dates, total interest and your debt-free date.
  • Savings goals: months to go at your current contribution, the monthly amount needed, and On track, Behind or Reached.
  • Annual dashboard: income, spending by bucket, net and savings rate month by month, a category-by-month grid against budget, and headline totals.
  • Light and dark mode. Transactions (1,500 rows), income (300), categories (40) and bills (40).
