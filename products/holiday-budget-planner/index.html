Holiday budget, gift tracker (Idea to Given), stockings, cards, meal and baking, Black Friday savings, a Nov-Dec calendar and a fairness view. Light and dark mode.

Set one holiday budget, split it across gifts, stockings, cards, events, the big meal and decorations, and see where you stand at any point in the season. It works the same in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets, with no macros and nothing to install.

What's inside

  • Dashboard: total budget, spent, remaining, planned (ideas included), Black Friday savings and days until Christmas, plus spending against budget for each category.
  • Recipients: a per-person budget for everyone you buy for, grouped (Family, Kids, Friends, Work…), compared with last year.
  • Gift tracker: each gift moves Idea → Bought → Wrapped → Given, with estimated and actual price, store, order and arrival dates.
  • Stockings, Cards (with a Sent column), Events & hosting, Meal & baking shopping list, Decor & extras.
  • Black Friday & Cyber Monday tracker: regular vs sale price, saving and % off, with each deal labeled Good deal, Small discount or No saving against your own threshold.
  • November–December calendar: key dates (US Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Small Business Saturday, Cyber Monday, Giving Tuesday, Christmas Eve and Day, Boxing Day, New Year's Eve) worked out for whatever year you enter, plus every dated item from your tabs.
  • Fairness view: compares each person's total with their group's average and flags anyone well above or below, so the kids stay even.
  • Light and dark mode with one dropdown.
  • Reusable every year: change one Year cell and the dates, calendar and countdown follow.
