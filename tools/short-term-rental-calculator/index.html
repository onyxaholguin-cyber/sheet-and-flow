Year-one estimate for education only, not financial advice.

How it calculates

  • Booked nights = (365 − owner-blocked nights) × occupancy. Stays = booked nights ÷ average stay.
  • Gross revenue = booked nights × nightly rate + stays × cleaning fee charged.
  • Operating expenses = gross × (host fee + management + maintenance/CapEx %) + stays × cleaning cost + 12 × fixed monthly costs.
  • Cash flow = NOI − 12 × loan payment (P&I). Break-even occupancy is the occupancy where cash flow is zero at your nightly rate.

Same formulas as the open-source Sheet & Flow Landlord Math tools, checked against them by an automated test. For long-term rentals use the rental property calculator.
